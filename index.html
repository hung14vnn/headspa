Before Your Arrival

The Art of
Preparation.

A few gentle rituals to ensure your visit unfolds seamlessly — from your first calming breath to your radiant departure.

✦ Arrive & Unwind

Complimentary organic herbal teas, infused mineral waters, and private lockers await you in our relaxation lounge.

01

Arrive 5–10 min early

Check in calmly and enjoy complimentary drinks before your therapist begins your ritual.

02

Dress comfortably

Wear relaxed clothing and minimize jewelry for a peaceful, completely unhurried experience.

03

Skip washing hair

Avoid shampooing before your visit so our scalp analysis can clearly showcase your before & after results.

04

No styling products

Please refrain from applying hairspray, gels, dry shampoos, or heavy oils prior to your treatment.

05

Detangle beforehand

Gently brush and detangle your hair before arriving so we can begin your treatment smoothly.

06

Share allergies

Inform us in advance of any sensitivities. Guests with reactive scalps are welcome to bring their own products.

07

After your ritual

We recommend avoiding coloring or chemical hair treatments immediately following your deep scalp session.

08

Safe during pregnancy

All head spa rituals are completely pregnancy-safe, provided you are comfortable lying back for about an hour.
